\section{Conclusion}
We proposed a freeway advisory agent that combines short-horizon ETC speed forecasts with official traffic-control notices and verifies numeric claims in its response. Preliminary results on National Freeway No.~5 show that gradient boosting can reduce speed errors, with smaller gains during holidays. Persistence achieved higher congestion-onset detection rates at the evaluated alert budgets. The next evaluation will test generalization across holiday periods, calibrate alerts prospectively, and assess whether the agent's source-grounded advice improves departure decisions.
